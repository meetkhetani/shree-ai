About Shree AI

Young minds from Kutch, building the future of AI

Our Story

Shree AI was born from a simple belief: artificial intelligence should be accessible to everyone, not just tech giants and researchers.

Founded in early 2024 by three passionate 19-year-old students from Kutch, Gujarat — Meet Khetani, Meet Joshi, and Prit Dudhakiya — we started Shree AI in a small room with nothing but laptops and big dreams. We saw how AI was transforming industries globally, but most tools were either too expensive, too complex, or both.

So we built Shree AI — a platform that democratizes AI technology, making it simple, affordable, and powerful for businesses of all sizes. We're still in our early stages, currently running beta programs and onboarding our first users.

Our journey from a small town in Gujarat to building AI tools for users around the world proves that innovation knows no boundaries. We're just getting started.

From Kutch to the World

Our Mission

To democratize artificial intelligence by creating powerful, user-friendly tools that empower individuals and businesses to solve real-world problems with AI.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted AI platform, where anyone can build, deploy, and scale intelligent solutions without barriers of complexity or cost.

Our Values

Innovation, accessibility, integrity, and community. We believe in building technology that serves humanity and creates opportunities for all.
